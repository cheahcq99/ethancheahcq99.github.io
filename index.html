About Me

Data Engineer

Ethan began his career at Swap Asia as a Data Analyst, where he developed his foundation in data reconciliation, data quality, and business analytics. Working with large operational datasets taught him the importance of data integrity and how reliable data can influence business decisions.

He then joined Turnkey Consulting, which became the turning point in his career. While working as a Data Analyst, he was exposed to ETL development, Python automation, SQL, BigQuery, and Power BI. More importantly, this was where he discovered his passion for data engineering — essentially, love at first sight. Building pipelines, automating repetitive processes, and understanding how data moves through systems sparked an interest that went beyond analytics.

In 2025, Ethan transitioned into Data Engineering at Hytech, where he began working extensively with Databricks, PySpark, Delta Lake, and Medallion Architecture. His work evolved toward architecting scalable pipelines, optimising large-scale data processing, and engineering reliable data infrastructure across datasets exceeding 50 million records.

Today, Ethan focuses on building the upstream data infrastructure that powers downstream analytics. His pipelines transform raw and operational data into structured, reliable datasets that data analysts and business users can confidently consume for reporting, analytics, and decision-making.
